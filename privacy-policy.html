Sea Lawyer Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Sea Lawyer is an independent reference app. It is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with the U.S. Navy, the Department of Defense, the Department of War, or any other government agency.

Short version

Sea Lawyer does not require an account, run ads, sell or rent personal information, or build advertising profiles. The reference library works without sending your questions to Sea Lawyer. Saved answers, preferences, and general question notes you deliberately save stay in the app's local storage.

If Sea Lawyer offers Help us find what the library misses?, you may separately choose to share screened searches. Sharing is off until you opt in, happens only after you submit a search or tap a suggestion, and can be turned off in the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores its reference library and may store your saved answers, Quick Draw shortcuts, display and answer preferences, setup acknowledgement, search-sharing choice, cached source-status information, and general unanswered questions you explicitly choose to save. On supported Apple devices, the app may also cache locally verified StoreKit entitlement evidence so a completed Sea Lawyer Plus purchase can be restored or recognized offline.

This local information is used to operate the app on your device. Sea Lawyer does not receive a copy of your saved answers, Quick Draw shortcuts, preferences, or local question notes. Local question notes are separate from optional search sharing and are never sent through the search-demand intake.

You can remove this local information by using an available in-app clear option, clearing the app's data through your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Your operating-system or cloud-backup settings may retain app data according to Apple, Google, or your backup provider's policies.

Help us find what the library misses

The App Store production release does not offer or connect this optional search-sharing feature. The following description applies only to versions that explicitly offer it.

This optional feature is available only when the app shows you the choice and its private intake is connected. If you opt in, Sea Lawyer waits until you submit a search or tap an app suggestion. It does not transmit keystrokes, typing pauses, saved answers, local question notes, or payment information.

Before anything is queued, the app normalizes the phrase and automatically rejects searches shaped like operational details, contact information, identifiers, names, addresses, unit identifiers, or sensitive personal cases. Automated screening can miss details, so never type private, medical-case, legal-case, restricted, controlled, or operational information.

The optional upload contains the screened phrase; whether it was typed or suggested; a bounded result type, match quality, and answer or publication count; up to three matching Sea Lawyer receipt identifiers; app and offline-library versions; a local aggregate count; and a random one-use retry identifier. Sea Lawyer does not add a name, email address, account, advertising identifier, or stable app-generated user, device, or session identifier.

Unsent search counts are stored on your device. When the app queues or prepares an upload, it removes aggregates whose most recent matching search is more than 14 days old. Repeating a matching search refreshes that period, and data can remain longer while the app is inactive. Turning sharing off stops new uploads and immediately clears every unsent count. The intake service increments daily phrase totals without retaining an individual search-event row. One-use retry identifiers expire after 14 days, below-reporting-threshold counters expire after 14 days, and reportable aggregate demand is retained for up to 90 days. Because no account or stable identifier is attached, maintainers do not have a reliable way to identify or individually delete one person's contribution after it has been combined. A phrase that contains identifying detail could still expose that detail; automated screening is imperfect, so do not enter it.

Combined search demand is visible only to Sea Lawyer's maintainers. It can help decide which missing question needs official research, but popularity never supplies, changes, or verifies an answer. Official public authority and Sea Lawyer's receipt checks still control every answer.

Network requests

Sea Lawyer periodically downloads small public files that report publication status and public question trends. Those requests do not include your search text, saved answers, or local question notes. If you opt into search sharing, a separate intake request contains only the fields described above.

Like most web hosts, the hosting provider necessarily processes standard connection information such as an IP address, date and time, device or browser type, and security or delivery logs. Sea Lawyer does not add that connection information to its search-demand database or use it to build user profiles.

App updates

Sea Lawyer uses Expo Application Services (EAS Update) to check for and deliver app updates. Update requests may include the operating system, app runtime and update channel, Sea Lawyer project identifier, and a random installation token used to determine whether an installation has requested or downloaded an update. These requests do not include your search text, saved answers, or local question notes.

Expo processes update requests and related service information under its privacy policy. Its end-user privacy explanation describes the operating-system information and random token used for updates. Sea Lawyer does not use this update information for advertising or combine it with your searches.

Purchases

On supported Apple devices, Apple processes Sea Lawyer Plus purchases through the App Store and StoreKit. Sea Lawyer may request the localized price, start a purchase, restore purchases, and check whether the entitlement remains valid. Sea Lawyer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple's privacy policy and your App Store settings govern the information Apple processes.

How information is shared

Sea Lawyer does not sell or rent personal information and does not share it for targeted advertising. Information is disclosed only as needed for the app functions described here: to hosting, update, or intake providers that deliver public status files, app updates, or optional screened-search aggregates, to Apple for App Store purchase handling, when you choose to open an external site, or when disclosure is required by law.

Official and third-party links

When you choose to open an official source or another external link, it opens outside Sea Lawyer. The destination site receives the normal information associated with a web request and applies its own privacy policy. Sea Lawyer does not control those sites.

Sensitive information and OPSEC

Do not enter or share classified, controlled, restricted, operational, medical-case, personally identifying, or other sensitive information. Do not include movements, schedules, locations, unit readiness, rosters, watch details, names, contact information, or identification numbers. Keep questions general and use only information already released by an official source.

Children

Sea Lawyer is intended for service members, veterans, and other adult users. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Sea Lawyer's data practices materially change, this page will be updated before or when the change is released, and a new consent choice will be requested when required. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on Sea Lawyer's official app-store listing. Technical issues with the public source-status service may also be reported through the public issue tracker.
