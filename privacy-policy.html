Sea Lawyer Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Sea Lawyer is an independent reference app. It is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with the U.S. Navy, the Department of Defense, the Department of War, or any other government agency.

Short version

Sea Lawyer does not require an account, run ads, sell personal information, or use advertising or analytics trackers. Searches, saved answers, preferences, and unanswered questions stay in the app's local storage unless you choose to share them outside the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores its reference library and may store your saved answers, display preferences, setup acknowledgement, access setting, cached source-status information, and unanswered questions you explicitly choose to save. This information is used only to operate the app on your device. Sea Lawyer does not receive a copy.

You can remove this local information by using an available in-app clear option, clearing the app's data through your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Your operating-system or cloud-backup settings may retain app data according to Apple, Google, or your backup provider's policies.

Network requests

Sea Lawyer periodically downloads small public files that report publication status and public question trends. The app does not include your search text or saved questions in those requests. Like most web hosts, the hosting provider may process standard connection information such as an IP address, date and time, device or browser type, and request logs for security and delivery. Sea Lawyer does not use that information to build user profiles.

Official and third-party links

When you choose to open an official source or another external link, it opens outside Sea Lawyer. The destination site receives the normal information associated with a web request and applies its own privacy policy. Sea Lawyer does not control those sites.

Sensitive information and OPSEC

Do not enter or share classified, controlled, restricted, operational, medical-case, personally identifying, or other sensitive information. Do not include movements, schedules, locations, unit readiness, rosters, watch details, names, contact information, or identification numbers. Keep questions general and use only information already released by an official source.

Children

Sea Lawyer is intended for service members, veterans, and other adult users. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If Sea Lawyer's data practices materially change, this page will be updated before or when the change is released. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on Sea Lawyer's official app-store listing. Technical issues with the public source-status service may also be reported through the public issue tracker.
